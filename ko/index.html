Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains what information the Slide Lines game (the "App") uses, why, who receives it and what choices you have. It applies to the App on Android and iOS. Slide Lines is first being released in Israel; if we offer it in other countries, we will update this policy where needed.

1. Who we are

Slide Lines is made by Ofir Goren, an independent developer in Israel. Ofir Goren decides how your information is used (the "data controller"). "We" and "us" mean Ofir Goren.

Contact: ofirstudio.support@gmail.com

If you contact us, we receive your email address, any name shown in your email, and the information you choose to include, so that we can respond to your inquiry or privacy request. Providing this information is voluntary; without a way to contact you or enough detail, we may be unable to respond or resolve your request. Support correspondence is separate from the App's analytics data.

2. At a glance

  • You can play without an account. There is no sign-in, chat or in-app purchase.
  • Your saved progress and settings are stored on your device, subject to device backups (Section 3). We do not run a game server, and this saved data is not sent to us. Gameplay events, such as scores, levels played and settings changes, are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase, and we can see them in its reports (Section 5).
  • The App shows ads from Google AdMob and uses Google Analytics for Firebase to improve the game. Google receives the information described below.
  • The App does not ask for your name or email address, or access your contacts, photos or precise location. If you email us, we receive the information described in Section 1.
  • The App checks whether Google's consent message is required. Where Google requires privacy options, you can change your choices in Settings. A Google consent message is not shown in every country; analytics and ads may operate without that message (Sections 5 and 6).

3. Information on your device

The App saves the following on your device. This saved data is not sent to us. Gameplay events about the same activity, such as scores, levels completed and settings changes, are sent to Google Analytics for Firebase as described in Section 5.

  • Scores, game statistics and level progress.
  • Sound, music and language settings, and whether you have seen the tutorial.
  • Whether you agreed to reminders, and counters that limit how often ads and hints appear.
  • Your choices in Google's consent message.
  • Google's software in the App (AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase and Google's consent tools) also keeps its own settings and identifiers on your device.
  • Where analytics is allowed, a random installation identifier, a session count and the date you first opened the App.

Uninstalling the App or clearing its data removes the local copy of this information. If backup is turned on for your device or account, your device's operating system may include some of this information in a backup and restore it later, for example on a new device. A restored backup can bring back earlier settings, such as your consent choices and, on Android, the App's installation identifier. Removing the local copy does not necessarily remove backup copies; these are managed through your device's backup settings, not by us.

4. Ads (Google AdMob)

The App shows rewarded ads, which you choose to watch, for example to continue a game. Some test versions of the App, including versions used for closed testing, also offer rewarded hints and full-screen ads between levels, shown no more often than a set limit. The App has no banner ads. We ask Google to show only ads rated suitable for teenagers or younger (Google's "Teen" rating or lower). Test versions of the App show Google's test ads, but Google's ad software still collects the information below.

An ad can be requested and loaded before you choose to watch it. Choosing not to watch a rewarded ad therefore does not, by itself, prevent the ad software's data collection.

Google's ad software collects the following and shares it with Google to show and measure ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud:

  • your IP address, which can indicate your approximate location;
  • your interactions with the App and with ads, such as taps and video views;
  • diagnostic information, such as app performance;
  • device identifiers, such as the Android advertising ID and the app set ID; on iOS, app- or developer-specific identifiers may be used even when the advertising identifier is unavailable.

Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choices, your device settings and Google's policies. On iOS, the App does not ask for permission to track you, so the Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) is not available to the App or to Google. Google explains how it uses this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and in the Google Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played and to improve it. It collects:

  • game events the App sends, such as games and levels started, finished or failed, scores, tutorial progress, settings changes, reminder choices, and whether ads loaded, were shown or failed;
  • events that Firebase and Google's ad software record automatically, such as the first time you open the App, sessions and time spent in the App, App and operating-system updates, and ad requests and impressions;
  • how the App was installed, for example from Google Play;
  • technical details, such as the App version, platform, device model, operating system version and language;
  • random identifiers created by the App and by Firebase; the App does not attach your name, contact details or advertising ID to its gameplay events;
  • your approximate location (country or city), which Google works out from your IP address.

The App configures Analytics not to collect the advertising ID, the Android device ID or the iOS identifier for vendor (IDFV). It does not set Firebase's User-ID field, but does send the random identifiers described above. The App also disables Analytics advertising consent signals.

We can view the collected events in Google Analytics, both in summary reports and in tools that show the events of a single installation. The gameplay events the App sends are linked to the random identifiers above. This lets us distinguish installations and their activity, but does not by itself tell us your name or contact details.

Where Google's European consent message applies, the App uses your choices about device storage and content measurement to enable or disable analytics. While the check runs, game events may wait temporarily in memory and are sent if analytics is then enabled. When those choices do not allow analytics, the App disables collection and deletes its installation identifier. If you change from allowing to refusing analytics in PRIVACY OPTIONS, the App also requests a reset of Firebase's analytics data on your device. Firebase saves its on/off setting between launches. In some cases, for example if the App closed unexpectedly, Firebase may briefly resume collection on the next launch before the App finishes checking your current choices.

Outside that European consent flow, including when Google reports that no message is required, analytics may be enabled without an analytics consent prompt. Google may also use analytics data as its own policies and our account's data-sharing settings allow: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy.

6. Your choices

  • Consent message. When the App starts, it checks with Google whether a consent message applies to you and asks Google to show it when required. Ads are requested only when Google's SDK reports that requests are allowed; this can include using a previously saved choice. Analytics follows the behaviour described in Section 5.
  • Privacy options. Where Google requires this option, Settings includes PRIVACY OPTIONS. Use it to review or change the choices Google makes available. The App applies the resulting analytics setting and requests the corresponding change in Firebase. When you complete the form, the App discards any ad that was loaded, or was still loading, before the change instead of showing it. A new ad is requested only if Google's SDK allows requests, and the SDK applies your updated choices to it. An ad already on screen is not interrupted.
  • Advertising ID on Android. You can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (usually Settings > Google > Ads, or Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • iOS. The App does not ask for tracking permission. You can manage ad settings in the iOS Settings app.
  • Reminders. If you agree and allow notifications, the App schedules a reminder on your device after some days without playing. The reminder is created on your device and is not sent through a server. Where analytics is allowed, your reminder choices and whether a reminder was scheduled or opened are recorded as analytics events (Section 5). You can turn reminders off in your device's notification settings.

Analytics and ads are separate. Refusing or withdrawing analytics does not stop Google's ad software from collecting the information in Section 4 to show, measure and protect ads, and ads, including limited or non-personalised ads, may still be shown. Your choices can limit how Google uses that information. The App has no separate switch to turn off analytics in locations where these privacy options are unavailable. Resetting an advertising ID does not turn off analytics or all ad data collection.

7. Why we use information

We use information to show ads (including the rewarded ads you choose), measure them and prevent fraud; to understand and improve the game; to keep your progress and settings; to send a reminder you agreed to; to respond to support and privacy requests; and to comply with the law.

You do not need an account or to send us contact details to play. Watching a rewarded ad is optional; if you do not watch it, you do not receive that reward. Analytics and ad software may still collect information as described above. The availability of Google's consent message and privacy options is described in Sections 5 and 6. Reminders are scheduled only if you agree and allow notifications.

You are not required by law to provide any information to use the App. If you refuse a choice that the App or Google asks you to make, such as analytics, personalised ads or reminders, you can still play: ads may be less relevant, analytics is not collected where your choice prevents it, and reminders are not scheduled.

8. Sharing and international transfers

Information is shared as follows:

  • Google, which provides AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase (Sections 4 and 5). Google may also use ad information for its own purposes and process information through service providers and advertising partners under its policies. Our support email is also hosted by Google (Gmail).
  • Authorities or others, when the law requires it or to protect rights and safety.

We are based in Israel. Google may process information in the United States and other countries. Google describes its safeguards for international transfers at https://policies.google.com/privacy/frameworks.

9. Keeping, deleting and protecting information

  • On your device: the local copy is kept until you uninstall the App or clear its data, which you can do at any time. Backup copies are subject to your device's backup settings (Section 3).
  • Google Analytics: our Google Analytics settings keep event-level data for 2 months and user-level data, such as the random identifiers and the information linked to them, for 14 months. New activity from the same installation restarts the 14-month period. Google deletes expired data periodically. These settings do not cover aggregated reports, which may be kept longer.
  • Google AdMob: kept according to Google's retention policy (https://policies.google.com/technologies/retention).

The App has no accounts, but analytics events can be linked to an installation through the random identifiers described in Section 5. Where available, withdrawing the choices that allow analytics in PRIVACY OPTIONS disables analytics and deletes the App's installation identifier. It does not delete information already sent. Google Analytics applies its retention settings to the data those settings cover; information collected by Google AdMob is kept and deleted by Google under its own retention policy. For a request about information already collected, contact us as described in Section 11. The analytics retention periods do not apply to support emails.

Google states that information sent by its ad and analytics software is encrypted in transit. Information on your device is kept in the App's private storage. No system is completely secure.

10. Teenagers and children

  • The App is for people aged 13 and older. It is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
  • The App does not ask your age and does not itself apply different privacy settings to players aged 13 to 17. Google may apply its own protections for teenagers it identifies under its policies. Separately, for all players, we ask Google to show only ads with a content rating suitable for teenagers or younger (Google's "Teen" rating or lower). This limits the kind of ad content shown; it does not change what information is collected or whether ads are personalised.
  • Some countries give young people extra protection, or set a higher age for agreeing to the use of their data. If you are a teenager, please read this policy with a parent or guardian.
  • If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child under 13 has used the App, contact us at ofirstudio.support@gmail.com. Because there are no accounts, we may not be able to identify the child's information, but we will explain what we can do.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to get a copy of it, to object to or restrict some uses of it, to opt out of targeted advertising, and to withdraw your consent at any time (in Settings > PRIVACY OPTIONS, where available). You may also complain to a data protection authority.

If you are in Israel, Israeli privacy law gives you, among other things, the right to review information about you that is held in a database, and to ask that information that is incorrect, incomplete, unclear or out of date be corrected or deleted.

To make a request, email ofirstudio.support@gmail.com. We will reply and explain what we can do. Your email address alone may not let us find your installation's analytics or ad information. If we cannot identify the relevant information, we will explain that limitation and consider any additional information you provide that could help us locate it. The App does not currently offer an in-app request to delete information already sent to Google. For information associated with a Google Account that Google controls, you can also use https://myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy; this does not replace a request to us about the App's analytics.
